Privacy - private Google Drive copy tool

Last updated October 12, 2026

This private tool (shown on the Google sign-in screen as "rclone") is used only by Stephen Lee, the owner of this website, to copy his own family photos, videos and documents between his own computer, his own backup disks and his own Google Drive.

It is not offered to anyone else. It collects no information about anyone, keeps no data of its own, and shares nothing with anyone. Files stay in Stephen Lee's own Google Drive and on his own devices.

Access to Google Drive is used only to copy, move and check his own files. Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account settings under "Third-party apps and services".

Questions: please contact the owner of this website, Stephen Lee.
