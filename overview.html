  • A solid arm: a lit branch
    實線枝子：已點亮的枝
  • A dimmer arm: a branch with no link
    較暗的枝：沒有連結
  • The cup with its number: the branch number
    杯上的數字：枝的編號
  • A knop (Exod 25:34): four on the shaft, each energized by the Holy Spirit
    球：幹上四個，都靠聖靈運行
  • The collar, Seen and Treasured (Ps 119:18, 119:11), not a knop: carries the lowest pair 10 and 11
    環：看見與珍藏（詩 119:18、119:11），不是球：托著最下面的 10、11 兩枝
  • The base: the Lord Jesus Christ, the cornerstone (1 Cor 3:11)
    座：主耶穌基督，房角石（林前 3:11）
  • Plus, minus, ⟲ reset, 3D (drag to turn); wheel and keys + - 0 and arrows zoom and move; drag a lamp out; a plain click opens it
    放大、縮小、⟲ 還原、3D（拖動旋轉）；滾輪與按鍵也可；可把燈拖開；單擊打開
  • Point to a lamp, an arm, a knop, the collar or the base: a short card (on a phone a tap; a second tap on a lamp goes in)
    指向燈、枝、球、環或座：一張短卡（手機點一下；在燈上再點一下進入）
  • The scroll under the base: Thy word, the lamp (Ps 119:105); the preacher sent (Rom 10:14-15)
    座下的書卷：你的話是燈（詩 119:105）；奉差遣傳道（羅 10:14-15）

And one of the elders saith unto me, Weep not: behold, the Lion of the tribe of Juda, the Root of David, hath prevailed to open the book, and to loose the seven seals thereof. (Revelation 5:5, KJV)

我又看見寶座與四活物，並長老之中有羔羊站立，像是被殺過的，有七角七眼，就是神的七靈，奉差遣往普天下去的。（啟示錄 5:6 和合本）

And I beheld, and, lo, in the midst of the throne and of the four beasts, and in the midst of the elders, stood a Lamb as it had been slain, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of God sent forth into all the earth. (Revelation 5:6, KJV)
